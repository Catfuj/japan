The deal

88kg → 76–77kg by wheels-up (Sat 21 Nov — week 19 ends Fri 20th, and yesterday's full body opened week 1). That's ~0.6kg a week — steady enough to keep muscle, energy and adherence. Week 10 review: if you're ahead of the corridor and feeling strong, we tighten the target then. Not before.

Phases

Non-negotiables

  • Sleep: in bed 22:00, up 05:30. This is the highest-leverage habit — it protects everything else.
  • Smoke-free throughout. The single biggest energy fix you can make. Expect weeks 1–3 to feel worse before better — that's withdrawal, not the plan failing.
  • Alcohol cap: max 2 occasions/week, max 2 drinks each, ≥5 alcohol-free days. An AF beer with colleagues counts as alcohol-free — and keeps the cycle home.
  • Cycling: ≥3 commute legs/week (Mon + Fri, ~45 min each way). 4 is a bonus, 3 is the bar.
  • Weigh-in: Sunday morning, after loo, before breakfast. Log it in Track. One number a week — daily readings are noise.

Missed a session?

Make it up Sunday morning — do the session you missed. If you missed two, do one Saturday-style full body session and let the other go. Never stack two sessions to "catch up"; the week resets Monday and three good sessions beat four resentful ones.

Nutrition targets

  • Weeks 1–2: ~2,000–2,100 kcal. Gentle on purpose — nicotine withdrawal raises appetite; we don't fight two wars at once.
  • Week 3 on: ~1,850–1,950 kcal.
  • Protein: 140g/day. Anchor every meal around it. This is what keeps you full and keeps muscle on.
  • Fibre: 30g+/day — beans, lentils, veg, oats, edamame. Also a satiety weapon.
  • Fed training: small bite before every morning session (banana, or a few spoons of yoghurt, or a small glass of milk). Proper breakfast after.

Book the blood panel

One GP appointment: full blood count, ferritin, thyroid (TSH), HbA1c, vitamin D. If the fatigue has a physiological driver, we want to know by week 10, not week 19. Book it this week.

A standard week

Morning training for the first fortnight, then review. If you're dragging by week 2, the fix is moving Wednesday to an evening session — not more caffeine.

Every evening, same shape: dinner → 15-min tidy → Japanese Netflix with red light mask (10–20 min) + supplements → lights dim 21:30 → bed 22:00. The mask and the show are the treat that ends the day, not another chore.

How to progress

Double progression: when you hit the top of the rep range on all sets with a rep or two left in the tank, add 2.5kg next session. If a session isn't finished in the time you have, cut the last exercise, not the first — the big lifts are the session.
